== RFCs for Volt

This repository contains *Request for Comments (RFCs)* for substantial
changes, new features, or significant design decisions in *Volt*.

The RFC process provides a consistent, transparent, and community-driven
path for evolving the project. Major changes should go through an RFC to
gather feedback, build consensus, and ensure thoughtful design before
implementation.

*This RFC process is directly based on the standards and practices
defined by the Rust team* (see
https://github.com/rust-lang/rfcs[rust-lang/rfcs]), which is widely
regarded as one of the most effective and mature RFC processes in open
source.

=== Why RFCs?

* Encourage open discussion and diverse perspectives.
* Document design rationale for future reference.
* Avoid surprising or poorly coordinated changes.

Small bug fixes, documentation improvements, or minor refactors do not
require an RFC.

=== Pre-RFC Feedback (Strongly Recommended)

Before opening a formal RFC, *please seek early feedback* from the
community or maintainers. This helps refine ideas, catch potential
issues early, and increase the chances of a smooth RFC process.

You can discuss your idea via: - *Slack* (in the relevant channels) -
*Email* or direct message to maintainers - An informal issue in the main
Volt repository - A casual conversation — even a dinner date, coffee
chat, or voice call!

The goal is to socialize the concept and get initial reactions before
investing time in a full RFC document.

=== How to Propose an RFC

[arabic]
. *Fork this repository*.
. Copy `0000-template.md` to `proposals/0000-my-feature.md` (use a
descriptive title; don’t assign a number yet—the PR number will become
the RFC ID).
. Fill in the template with:
* Motivation
* Detailed proposal
* Drawbacks and alternatives
* Unresolved questions
. Submit a pull request. The PR discussion is where feedback and
iteration happen.
. Build consensus: Respond to comments, update the proposal as needed.
. Once merged, the RFC becomes "`active`" and can be implemented (with a
tracking issue in the main Volt repo if desired).

Put care into your RFC—clear motivation, impact analysis, and
consideration of alternatives lead to better reception.

=== Active and Accepted RFCs

* Merged RFCs are listed in the `proposals/` directory (renamed with
their assigned number).

For more details on the process, see the link:0000-template.md[RFC
template].

Contributions to the RFC process itself are welcome!
